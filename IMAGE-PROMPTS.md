# Gemini image prompts

Generate in Gemini, download, rename exactly as shown, and drop into `public/images/` (replace the placeholders).
Keep JPG, under ~400 KB each (compress at squoosh.app if larger).

## 1. `about-main.jpg` — portrait 6:7 (e.g. 1200×1400)
> Professional photograph of a modern document clearing / typing centre office in Dubai, UAE. A friendly Emirati male customer-service officer in a white kandura and ghutra sits behind a clean white service counter with a computer, helping a customer with passport and visa papers. Bright, airy interior with subtle green accents and a small UAE flag on the desk. Natural daylight, shallow depth of field, corporate and trustworthy mood, high detail, photorealistic, no text, no logos.

Clean white desk, soft green background blur, professional lighting, no logos, no faces.

## 4. Optional — one image per service category (square 1:1, 1080×1080, for Instagram posts)
Use this template, change the bracket part:
> Clean 3D illustration in soft clay style, white background with light green accents: [SUBJECT]. Professional, friendly, minimal, UAE government-services theme, small UAE flag detail, no text, no logos.

- MOHRE / Labour → "a worker's ID card, a contract document with a green check mark and a briefcase"
- GDRFA / ICP visas → "a passport with a visa page, an airplane and a residence card"
- Dubai Police reports → "a passport with a magnifying glass and an official stamped certificate"
- Embassy services → "a travel document (white passport) with an embassy building in the background"
- Typing & translation → "a computer keyboard, documents with Arabic and English lines, and a translation icon"

## Tips
- Don't use photos of real public figures or real government logos.
- Ask Gemini for "no text" — AI text inside images is usually misspelled.
